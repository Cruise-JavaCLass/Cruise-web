The maritime animations in this directory (`cruise-ship.json`, `radar-beacon.json`, `ocean-waves.json`) are designed for the Cruise Management Platform.

- **Design Inspiration:** LottieFiles Maritime Transport Pack (https://app.lottiefiles.com/premium-assets/lottie/packs/featured/maritime-transport)
- **Palette Alignment:**
  - Electric Cyan: `#42E8F5`
  - Navigation Blue: `#4169FF`
  - Retro Amber: `#FFBD59`
  - Midnight Navy: `#10182A` / `#151F33`
- **Specification:** Bodymovin / Lottie v5.5.7 Open Vector Standard
- **Motion Accessibility:** Fully integrated with `<MaritimeLottie />` supporting `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` fallback to crisp static maritime SVG icons and server/offline fallbacks.
